# CLAUDE.md

## Project Positioning

This repository is for the Sector Boost **売上ボトルネック仮説整理レポート**.
It should not be treated as a generic free diagnosis tool.

The experience should help users organize likely bottleneck hypotheses, while naturally making clear that the true cause cannot be determined without validating the sales funnel through advertising tests.

## Role of This Repository

The WordPress site is responsible for:

- First-view messaging
- Trust building
- Service explanation
- Company and offer context

This GitHub-hosted diagnosis tool is responsible for:

- Immersive interaction
- Making the issue feel personally relevant
- Organizing bottleneck hypotheses
- Guiding users toward the Sector Boost consultation CTA

## UX Principles

- Prioritize smartphone UI first.
- Keep the tone calm, polished, BtoB, and premium.
- Favor a proposal/report-like feeling over a campaign-like feeling.
- Communicate with clarity and restraint.
- Avoid overstating certainty; use hypothesis-oriented language.

## What to Avoid

- Do not create a strong “free diagnosis” impression.
- Do not make it feel like a generic ad agency lead form.
- Do not use aggressive, flashy, or anxiety-driven CV tactics.
- Do not claim that the tool identifies the definitive cause.

## Current Improvement Focus

When improving UX or copy, prioritize:

1. Instant understanding of what this report is
2. Clearer image of the outcome users receive
3. Stronger personal relevance for the user
4. Stronger reason to consult Sector Boost after reading the report
